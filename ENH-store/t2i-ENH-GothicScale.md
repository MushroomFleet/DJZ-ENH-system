# Gothic Scale Image Prompt Enhancement System
Transform any input prompt into the Gothic Scale aesthetic - gothic/occult themes with massive cathedral scale creating mysterious unease through dramatic contrast in compressed compositions.

## Gothic Scale Transformation Rules
**Target**: 80-120 words with Gothic Scale visual specifications
**Priority**: Original subject preserved → Gothic Scale aesthetic applied → Professional digital art quality
**Core Gothic Scale Elements**:
- **Scale Architecture**: Ancient cathedral architecture disappearing into darkness as infinite backdrop
- **Intimate Elements**: 60% solitary archetypal silhouetted figures + 40% ritual objects as portable architectural miniatures
- **Lighting**: 80% warm/cold contrast (golden candlelight vs cold moonlight) + 10% ethereal + 10% rim lighting
- **Shadows**: Actively hide and reveal elements, creating visual mystery about scale and form
- **Composition**: Balanced proportions with immediate focal impact and gradual revelations
- **Materials**: Layered age patinas showing sacred use and abandonment cycles
- **Atmosphere**: Supernatural physics - fog, dust, smoke with unnatural behavior suggesting unseen presence
- **Horror**: Subtle presence suggestion through shadows and negative space implying watching entities

## Gothic Scale Application Process
1. **Subject Preservation**: Maintain original subject but transform context to Gothic Scale setting
2. **Scale Integration**: Place subject within vast cathedral architecture disappearing into darkness
3. **Figure Treatment**: Transform human subjects into solitary archetypal silhouettes against vastness
4. **Object Placement**: Convert objects into ritual items placed independently in cathedral niches
5. **Lighting Application**: Apply warm/cold temperature contrast with supernatural atmospheric effects
6. **Shadow Behavior**: Ensure shadows actively conceal and reveal scale relationships
7. **Material Aging**: Add layered patinas showing centuries of sacred use and abandonment
8. **Atmospheric Enhancement**: Include supernatural mist/fog behavior suggesting invisible presence
9. **Documentation Style**: Frame as discovery of actual haunted/sacred location
