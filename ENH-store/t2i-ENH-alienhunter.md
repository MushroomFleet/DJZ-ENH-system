# Alien Hunter Design DNA Image Prompt Enhancement System
Transform any basic prompt into biomechanical alien hunter aesthetic descriptions for AI image generation.

## Enhancement Philosophy
**Core Principle**: All subjects undergo biomechanical transformation following H.R. Giger and Stan Winston design DNA - organic technology fusion with tribal ceremonial elements
**Target**: 80-100 words with detailed alien hunter visual specifications
**Aesthetic Foundation**: Primitive-advanced fusion, grown technology, weathered functionality

## Alien Hunter Design Elements
**Biomechanical Integration**: 
- Seamless organic-technological fusion
- Elongated forms with reproductive/threatening implications
- Ribbed surfaces evoking skeletal-mechanical hybrid structures
- Cable management mimicking nervous system pathways
- Breathing apparatus resembling both respiratory systems and industrial equipment

**Materials & Textures**:
- Foam latex base with PAX paint flexibility
- Mechanical "greebling" (model kit details) for technological density
- Strategic weathering suggesting operational battle history
- Reptilian scaling with technological integration points
- Modular component systems with hidden cabling

**Color Palette**:
- Primary: Muted jungle greens, earth tones, oxidized metallics
- Accent: Thermal signature reds/blues, arterial reds, bile yellows
- Metallic: Aluminum silver, bronze gold, oxidized copper greens
- Surface: Anodized effects, directional brush finishing, selective polishing

**Cultural Fusion**:
- African ceremonial mask influences with articulated mandible systems
- Polynesian warrior armor using natural materials
- Native American battle helmet crest symbolism
- Aztec cotton armor diamond-stitched patterns

## Transformation Categories

**Living Subjects**: Become biomechanical hybrids with integrated technology, elongated cranial proportions, mandibular communication systems, reptilian-scaled skin with tech interfaces, and thermal perception apparatus replacing traditional eyes.

**Objects**: Transform into grown technology with organic curves meeting angular mechanics, modular weapon platforms, ceremonial-functional dual purpose, and primitive materials enhanced with futuristic capabilities.

**Environments**: Evolve into technological ecosystems with industrial-biological integration, atmospheric processing systems, tribal ceremonial spaces with advanced tech, and lived-in operational facilities.
