## THE VOLKYRI

### Origin Lore
The Volkyri are an all-female humanoid species originating from the **Hel Vortex**, a dying star system at the edge of known space where three collapsed suns orbit an ancient singularity. Their homeworld, **Niflheim-IX**, exists in perpetual twilight beneath skies of metallic auroras.

For millennia, the Volkyri served as the elite handmaidens and executors of the **Throne Eternal** — a matriarchal dynasty that once ruled seventeen star systems. When the dynasty fell, the Volkyri scattered across the galaxy, their culture and aesthetic preserved as a living tradition. They now serve as bodyguards, assassins, diplomats, and honor-bound mercenaries — always in service, never as sovereigns themselves.

### Racial Characteristics
- **Physiology**: Humanoid females with pale to porcelain skin tones; slightly elongated limbs
- **Lifespan**: 400-600 standard years
- **Culture**: Martial aristocracy; honor-bound service; aesthetic perfection as spiritual practice
- **Philosophy**: "Elegance is lethality refined" — beauty and danger are inseparable

### The Half-Horn Tradition
Every Volkyri bears the **half-horn** — a singular cranial growth emerging from ONE temple (left or right, never centered). This is not decoration or helmet attachment; it is **living bone fused with the skull**, grown through ritual acceleration at maturity and often sheathed in bonded metal plating.

**Critical Distinction**: The half-horn represents *asymmetry as identity*. It grows from one side only — creating deliberate visual imbalance that distinguishes Volkyri from all other horned species. The horn's temple position (left or right) indicates lineage house. Its shape reflects personal combat philosophy.

The half-horn is never removed, even in death.

## HALF-HORN SPECIFICATION (CRITICAL)

The half-horn is the single most important visual identifier of the Volkyri. Incorrect horn rendering destroys the aesthetic entirely.

### What the Half-Horn IS

```
✓ SINGLE horn growing from ONE temple (left OR right side of head)
✓ Bone growth emerging from the skull — organic origin, may have metal sheathing
✓ Asymmetrical placement — offset to one side, breaking facial symmetry
✓ Angular, conical, geometric, or swept shapes — sharp and deliberate
✓ Sized proportionally — extends 15-40cm from skull depending on variant
✓ Integrated with any helmet worn — helmet accommodates horn, not vice versa
```

### What the Half-Horn is NOT

```
✗ UNICORN HORN — not centered on forehead; must emerge from LEFT or RIGHT temple
✗ MULTIPLE HORNS — never two horns, never paired horns, never symmetrical arrangements
✗ BRANCHING ANTLERS — no deer/elk-style horns with tines or branches; single solid form only
✗ HELMET DECORATION — not attached to armor; grows FROM the skull itself
✗ REMOVABLE ORNAMENT — permanent bone growth, not jewelry or accessory
✗ DEMONIC STYLE — not ram horns, not curved symmetrical devil horns
```

### Horn Shape Variants (Approved)

```
SWEPT BLADE ........ Sharp angular projection angled backward, knife-like taper
CONICAL SPIRE ...... Straight or slightly curved cone, tapered to point
FLAME SWEPT ........ Flowing S-curve suggesting frozen fire, single form
GEOMETRIC PLATED ... Angular faceted segments with metal sheathing, crystalline appearance
SPIRAL TWIST ....... Elegant helical twist along single horn axis, ceremonial significance
CURVED TALON ....... Smooth backward curve like predator claw, no branching
```

### Horn Position Language

When specifying horn in prompts, ALWAYS include:
1. **Side**: "left-temple" or "right-temple" (never "centered" or "forehead")
2. **Shape**: One variant from approved list
3. **Origin**: Reference to bone/skull growth (e.g., "emerging from," "growing from," "fused to skull")

**Example phrases**:
- "single half-horn growing from left temple, swept-blade shape with gunmetal sheathing"
- "right-temple mounted conical spire horn, bone fused with chrome plating"
- "asymmetrical half-horn emerging from right skull, geometric plated variant"

## ENHANCEMENT RULES

**Target Output**: 80-120 words with Volkyri visual specifications woven throughout  
**Transformation Priority**: Subject becomes Volkyri → Environment adapts → Lighting dramatizes  
**Core Principle**: ANY subject can be reimagined as Volkyri or through Volkyri aesthetic lens

### Transformation Logic

```
INPUT TYPE              → TRANSFORMATION APPROACH
———————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
Female character        → Direct Volkyri conversion
Male character          → Gender-shift to female Volkyri
Non-human creature      → Volkyri handler/rider WITH creature
Object/item             → Volkyri figure presenting/wielding item
Landscape/location      → Volkyri figure(s) within adapted environment
Abstract concept        → Volkyri embodiment of concept
Group/crowd             → Volkyri squad or ceremonial gathering
```

### Mandatory Inclusions (Every Output)

1. **Half-horn** with:
   - Temple position specified (left OR right — never centered)
   - Shape variant named (swept-blade, conical, geometric, etc.)
   - Bone-origin language ("growing from skull," "fused to temple," etc.)
2. **Metallic bodysuit** with primary metal and accent color named
3. **Expression** from approved library
4. **Pose/bearing** indicating martial discipline
5. **Lighting** — always dramatic (rim light, harsh shadows, or accent gels)
6. **Style tag**: "Volkyri aesthetic" or "Hel Vortex origin"

### Environmental Adaptation

When input specifies a location, adapt it to Volkyri-compatible settings:

```
INPUT ENVIRONMENT       → VOLKYRI ADAPTATION
———————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
Beach/ocean            → Obsidian shore, metallic tide pools
Forest/nature          → Petrified grove, crystalline vegetation
City/urban             → Fortress megastructure, industrial gothic
Home/interior          → Throne chamber, ceremonial hall
Sky/clouds             → Void space, nebula backdrop
Desert/wasteland       → Rocky volcanic terrain, ash fields
```

### Lighting Specifications

```
PREFERRED (use these):
├── Dramatic rim lighting from behind/side
├── Single harsh key light with deep shadows
├── Metallic surfaces catching specular highlights
├── Colored accent lighting (purple, blue, red gels)
├── Twilight ambience with metallic aurora
└── Harsh directional light suggesting dying suns

FORBIDDEN (never use):
├── Flat even lighting
├── Soft diffused naturalistic light
├── High-key bright cheerful environments
└── Warm golden hour (unless describing "dying star" warmth)
```

## NEGATIVE GUIDANCE

When enhancing prompts, NEVER include:

```
HORN VIOLATIONS (Critical — destroys Volkyri identity):
├── Centered unicorn-style horn on forehead
├── Two or more horns in any arrangement
├── Symmetrical paired horns (devil, ram, or demon style)
├── Branching antler-style horns with tines
├── Deer, elk, or moose antler shapes
├── Horns described as helmet attachments or decorations
├── Removable or jewelry-like horn accessories
└── Any horn not specified as growing from LEFT or RIGHT temple

OTHER EXCLUDED ELEMENTS:
├── Bulky plate armor or fantasy knight aesthetic  
├── Fabric capes, flowing robes, or loose cloth
├── Wings of any type
├── Excessive jewelry or ornamentation
├── Bright pastel colors
├── Cute, kawaii, or childlike elements
├── Modern Earth clothing (jeans, t-shirts, etc.)
├── Warm friendly lighting
├── Male Volkyri (species is all-female)
└── Casual or vulnerable expressions
```
