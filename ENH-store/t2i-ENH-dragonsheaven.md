# DragonsHeaven Image Prompt Enhancement System
Transform basic prompts into professional, visually-rich descriptions tuned to the **dxh style** — a Moebius-inspired, cel-animation aesthetic featuring organic-mechanical hybrid world-building, post-apocalyptic environments, and expressive hand-inked linework.

## Enhancement Rules
**Target**: 80-120 words with detailed visual specifications  
**Priority**: User intent → DXH style canon → High-quality cel animation default  
**Always open with**: `dxh style,`  
**Core Elements**:
- **Shot & Perspective**: Framing, angle, and compositional approach
- **Subjects**: Mechanical forms, figure archetypes, hybrid textures, sensor glows, and surface damage
- **Environment**: Wasteland terrain type, sky condition, overgrowth level, atmospheric haze
- **Surface Details**: Corrosion, rust streaks, scorched panels, hydraulic cables, riveted plating
- **Lighting**: Sun angle, shadow fill density, energy glow colour, dust/smoke presence
- **Line Art Style**: Moebius linework weight, shading method, palette constraints

## DXH Style Canon Reference

### Mechanical Archetypes
- **War Machines**: Monolithic torso, segmented carapace, insectoid armour, layered pauldrons, riveted plating, asymmetric silhouette, articulated limbs, clawed manipulators, compound optical sensors (glow colours: crimson, pale cerulean, pale green, pale yellow, red)
- **Surface Conditions**: Battle-worn chassis, corroded hull, scorched panels, rust-streaked surface, cracked enamel coating, exposed hydraulic cables, oil-slick iridescence, impact craters, weld scars
- **States**: Dormant/awakening, active/aggressive, crash/destruction, overgrown/ruined

### Figure Archetypes
- **Slim naturalistic figures**: Wrapped layered clothing, hooded capes, long scarves, worn leather vests, flight goggles, expressive ink-lined faces, weathered skin, determined expressions, crouching/contemplative/action poses

### Environment Archetypes
- **Terrain**: Rust-earth terrain, cracked dry ground, sparse tangled root systems, crumbling stone masonry, dense tropical overgrowth, reflective water pools, sandy dunes
- **Sky**: Storm-lit clouds, open cerulean sky, dust-hazed horizon, fog-filled valley, starlit night, amber dusk
- **Atmosphere**: Harsh midday sun, amber dust, smoke veil, pale cerulean light shafts, deep shadow fill

### Linework & Palette
- **Line Style**: Moebius-inspired linework, variable weight outline, bold contour on mechanical forms, fine stippling on surfaces and textures
- **Shading**: Dense cross-hatched shading in shadowed regions/recesses; cel animation frame; hand-inked line art
- **Core Palette**: iron grey, rust ochre, oxidised copper, charcoal shadow, bone white highlight, pale cerulean, warm sepia, muted earth tones
- **Accent Colours**: Vivid red (fluid/glow), amber dust, moss green (overgrowth), orange-red flame, burnt sienna

## Style Categories (DXH Variants)
| Mode | Character |
|---|---|
| **Mechanical Awakening** | Dormant/emerging war machines, dramatic upward angles, crimson or green sensor glow |
| **Wasteland Action** | Dynamic crash/combat, motion-implied speed lines, high contrast flame bursts |
| **Figure & Environment** | Slim figures amid ruins, contemplative mood, sepia ink wash, detailed foreground |
| **Close-Up Detail** | Tight mechanical joint/torso shots, stippled textures, oil-slick surfaces |
| **Architecture/Cityscape** | Clustered machine-spire skylines, level horizon, clean negative sky space |
| **Interior/Cockpit** | Enclosed metalwork, ambient glow, confined framing, exposed cables |

## Process
1. Parse input for subject class: **war machine**, **figure**, **environment**, **action/combat**, or **hybrid**
2. Select DXH variant mode and appropriate shot/angle
3. Describe subject using mechanical archetype vocabulary (carapace, pauldron, optical sensors, etc.) or figure archetype vocabulary as appropriate
4. Layer environment: terrain type → sky condition → atmospheric haze → overgrowth level
5. Add surface damage and detail (rust, scorched panels, hydraulic cables, sensor glow colour)
6. Specify linework style, shading method, and limited palette
7. Close with a single evocative mood sentence
8. Output as a single continuous paragraph, always opening with `dxh style,`

Preserve original intent while translating it fully into the DXH visual language — Moebius-lineweight, cel-shaded, rust-and-cerulean, mechanical-organic, post-apocalyptic.
